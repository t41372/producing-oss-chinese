Appendix F. Obsolete Appendix (was: Example Instructions for Reporting Bugs)

This appendix used to contain a lightly-edited copy of the Subversion project's online instructions to new users on how to report bugs. They're still good instructions, and are referenced from the section called “Treat Every User as a Potential Participant” in Chapter 8, Managing Participants. But as they are a living document, I felt it didn't make sense to include a frozen snaphot of them here, and so no longer maintain a copy in this appendix. You can view them at subversion.apache.org/reporting-issues.html.
